These are the only channels we use. Anyone contacting you from a different number, a different Discord account, or a lookalike email is not us. We will never ask for your password, an OTP, or a screen-share — no matter what the reason sounds like.

Before you message us

Two of these three solve most tickets in under a minute:

  • Check the UID on your receipt. If it doesn't match your in-game profile, the diamonds went to a different account — tell us straight away.
  • Restart Free Fire. Diamonds sometimes land while the client is open and only appear after a fresh login.
  • Check the time. If it's been under five minutes, it is almost certainly still on its way.

What to include

  • Your order ID (FF-XXXXXX-XXXX).
  • Your Free Fire UID and region.
  • A screenshot of the payment confirmation from your bank or UPI app.
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.

Response times

  • Undelivered order — under 10 minutes, any hour.
  • Refund request — acknowledged within 2 hours, issued same day.
  • Everything else — within 12 hours.
