When you get a full refund

  • The diamonds did not arrive within 30 minutes of a confirmed payment.
  • Money left your account but the order never appeared in our system.
  • You were charged more than once for the same order.
  • You cancelled before delivery started.
  • We credited the wrong package, or credited the wrong amount.
  • We are unable to service your region for the package you selected.

In every one of these cases you get back the full amount you paid, taxes included. We do not deduct a gateway fee, a "processing charge", or a restocking cut.

The one case where we can't refund

Once diamonds have been successfully credited to the UID you entered, the order cannot be reversed. In-game currency cannot be withdrawn from a Free Fire account once it lands — not by us, and not by Garena. This includes an order delivered to a UID you typed incorrectly.

This is why the confirmation screen shows your UID back to you in large type before you pay. Please read it. If you notice a mistake before delivery starts, message us immediately and we will stop the order and refund you in full.

Automatic refunds

You should not have to chase us. If an order passes 30 minutes without delivery, our system flags it, we message you on the contact you provided, and the refund is issued without you filing anything.

How to request one

Send your order ID (it looks like FF-XXXXXX-XXXX and appears on screen and in your receipt email) to any of these:

  • support@diamondvault.com — best if you need a written record.
  • Discord — open a ticket in #support.

We acknowledge every refund request within two hours, at any hour.

How long the money takes to come back

  • UPI, PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm — usually within minutes, up to 24 hours.
  • Wallets — 1 to 3 working days.
  • Net banking — 3 to 5 working days.
  • Credit and debit cards — 5 to 7 working days, set by your bank rather than by us.

Refunds always go back to the original payment method. We cannot redirect a refund to a different account, card or UPI ID — that restriction is a fraud-prevention rule, not our preference.

Chargebacks

Please talk to us before raising a chargeback with your bank. A chargeback takes 45–90 days to resolve; we can usually settle the same issue the same day. If you have already raised one, tell us the case reference and we will submit the delivery evidence to your bank on your behalf.

Consumer rights

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 or any other Indian law that applies to you.
