Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 July 2026

The short version

We collect your Free Fire UID, your region, your email and a record of your payment. That's it. We do not collect, request, or store your Free Fire password or any login credential — there is no field for it anywhere on this site, and no member of our team will ever ask you for one.

Who we are

This site is operated by [Registered legal entity name], [registered address], India. For anything in this policy, write to support@diamondvault.com. We are an independent reseller and are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Garena.

What we collect

  • Free Fire UID — required. Identifies the account that receives the diamonds. A UID is public information already visible to anyone in your lobby.
  • Region — required. Routes the order to the correct game server.
  • Player nickname — optional. Used only to double-check we are crediting the right account before delivery.
  • Email address — collected by the payment provider and passed to us so we can send your receipt and order updates.
  • Payment metadata — the amount, the method used, a transaction reference and the outcome. We never receive your card number, UPI PIN, CVV or OTP; those are entered on your bank's or provider's own page and never pass through our servers.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser and device type, and timestamps. Used for fraud prevention, chargeback disputes and debugging.

What we never collect

Your Free Fire password. Your Facebook, Google, VK or Apple login. Any OTP. Screen-share or remote-access sessions. If a message claiming to be from us asks for any of these, it is not from us — please report it to support@diamondvault.com and we will help you.

Why we process it

  • To deliver the diamonds you paid for (performance of a contract).
  • To issue receipts and process refunds (legal obligation and contract).
  • To detect fraudulent orders and chargebacks (legitimate interest).
  • To answer your support messages (legitimate interest).

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Who we share it with

Only the parties needed to complete your order: our payment gateway, our top-up distribution partner, our email provider, and our hosting provider. Each receives only the minimum needed. We also disclose data where legally compelled by a valid order from an Indian authority.

How long we keep it

  • Order and payment records — eight years, as required for Indian tax and accounting law.
  • Support conversations — two years.
  • Server and technical logs — 90 days.

Security

All traffic is served over TLS 1.3 with HSTS. Order data is encrypted at rest. Access to production data is limited to staff who need it and is logged. Payment card data is handled entirely by PCI-DSS compliant providers and never reaches our systems.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email support@diamondvault.com from the address you used at checkout, or send us your order ID on WhatsApp. We respond within 30 days. Note that we cannot delete completed order and payment records before the statutory retention period ends.

Children

Our service is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 18, use this service only with the permission of a parent or guardian, and only with their payment method. If you believe a minor has made a purchase without consent, contact us and we will refund it.

Cookies

This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies. Your package, UID and payment selections exist only in your browser's memory for the duration of the visit and are sent to us only when you confirm an order.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects an active order, email you directly.

Questions about any of this? A human replies in under four minutes.
