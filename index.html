Built to be checked, not just trusted

Every claim below is something you can verify before you pay a rupee — and one thing we will never do, no matter who asks.

  • 100% secure payments

    Processed by PCI-DSS compliant gateways. Your card number and UPI PIN are entered on your bank's page and never reach us.

  • Instant delivery

    Orders are queued the moment payment clears. Median delivery time this week is 2m 14s.

  • Trusted by thousands

    Over 40,000 completed top-ups. Order history and receipts stay available in your email for two years.

  • Encrypted checkout

    TLS 1.3 end to end, HSTS enforced. We store your UID and order — nothing that could be used to sign in as you.

  • 24×7 support

    A human on Discord and email at any hour. Median first reply is under four minutes.

  • Safe & reliable

    Diamonds are credited through official top-up channels, so your account is never put at risk of a ban.

We will never ask for your password, OTP, or a screen-share. Not by email, not on Discord, not even if the message looks like it came from us. Anyone who does is running a scam — report them and we'll help.
